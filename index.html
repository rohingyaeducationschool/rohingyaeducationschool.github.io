Welcome to Rohingya Education School

Rohingya Education School is committed to supporting quality learning, language development, education, and meaningful community participation. Our aim is to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where students can learn with confidence, develop their knowledge and skills, and grow together. We believe that education is an important foundation for building a better future. Through learning opportunities, language practice, creative activities, educational programs, and community engagement, we encourage students to explore their potential, improve their communication skills, and develop positive values. Our school also aims to promote respect, cooperation, creativity, and lifelong learning. We are committed to supporting students, teachers, and the wider community by creating opportunities to learn, share knowledge, develop skills, and work together for a stronger and more educated community.
