Our Activities

Activities are an important part of the learning experience at Rohingya Education School. They help students develop knowledge, confidence, communication skills and cooperation.

Our activities are connected with education and the development of a positive and supportive learning environment.

Building a Learning Community

Education is more than classroom learning. Students also learn through participation, cooperation and interaction with others.

Our activities aim to create a welcoming environment where learners can participate, share ideas and develop useful skills.

Future Activities

As the school develops, additional educational, cultural, language, sports and community activities may be introduced according to students' needs and available opportunities.
