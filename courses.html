Our Courses

Educational courses and learning opportunities for students and learners.

Courses at Rohingya Education School

Our courses are designed to support students in developing academic knowledge, language skills and useful learning abilities.

Course information can be updated as new subjects and learning programs become available.

Available Learning Areas

📖

English

English language learning with a focus on reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar and communication.

➗

Mathematics

Mathematical learning and practice to help students develop problem-solving and numerical skills.

🔬

Science

Science learning that encourages students to understand the natural world through study and exploration.

🌍

General Education

Educational learning that supports general knowledge, study skills and personal development.

📝

Language & Literacy

Activities that support reading, writing, vocabulary and communication skills.

💻

Digital Learning

Learning about digital tools and basic technology skills where suitable resources are available.

Course Information

Course names, levels, schedules and other details can be added or updated when the school provides the relevant information.

Learning Approach

Courses can include classroom learning, practice, assignments, discussion and other educational activities according to the needs of students.

The goal is to help learners build understanding step by step and develop confidence in their studies.
