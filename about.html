About Rohingya Education School

A learning community dedicated to education, knowledge, language and a better future.

Who We Are

Rohingya Education School is an educational initiative focused on creating opportunities for learning, developing knowledge and supporting students in their educational journey.

We aim to provide a supportive learning environment where students can develop their academic knowledge, language skills, confidence and values.

Our Vision

Our vision is to contribute to a future where every learner has access to meaningful education and the opportunity to develop their knowledge, skills and potential.

Our Mission

Quality Education

To support students through structured learning and educational activities.

Language & Learning

To encourage language development, communication and a strong learning culture.

Community

To build a supportive educational community where learners and educators can grow together.

Our Values

Respect

We value respect for students, teachers, families and the wider community.

Learning

We encourage curiosity, continuous learning and the development of useful skills.

Responsibility

We encourage learners to become responsible, active and constructive members of society.

Educational Focus

Our educational activities are designed to support students in their studies and personal development.

The school website also provides information about courses, educational activities, updates and certificate verification.
